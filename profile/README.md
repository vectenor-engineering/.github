# Vectenor Engineering


Independent CAD, CAE, and AI consulting for automotive and mechanical engineering.


We support geometry preparation, simulation setup, numerical troubleshooting, and technical reporting. Our work spans focused engineering tasks and coordinated projects with agreed deliverables and review milestones.
